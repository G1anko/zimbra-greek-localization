# Contributing

Issues and translation improvements are welcome.

When reporting an untranslated or incorrect string, please include:
- Zimbra version;
- screenshot;
- exact location in the UI;
- suggested Greek wording, if you have one.

Do not post passwords, private email content, account identifiers, or other sensitive data.

Before adding or changing a translation:
- preserve placeholders such as `{0}` and `{1}`;
- preserve HTML/MessageFormat syntax;
- do not remove upstream copyright or license headers.
